Research

What we study, and how far it has got.

Work is organised as projects. Each one moves from a question to a proof of concept, and then to working research on a farm.

  1. 01Question

    Something we want to know, written down before we start.

  2. 02Experiment

    A small, controlled test with the readings logged.

  3. 03Proof of concept

    Does the idea work at all? We show it on a bench or a single run.

  4. 04Working research

    Repeated runs on a working farm, in real heat and humidity.

  5. 05Shared

    Protocols and data, published once they are checked.

01

Mushroom research

running

Our first and main area. Exotic oyster is being grown in Chennai with every run logged, and king oyster is next.

  • Strains: which ones cope with tropical heat and humidity, compared under the same logged conditions
  • Cultivation: runs repeated on a working farm, so results are not one-off
  • Spawn efficiency: how much spawn a run needs, and what quality and handling change
02

Cooling technology and HVAC

exploring

Keeping a growing room cool at low effort, where the weather is hot.

  • Ways to lower cooling effort in hot weather
  • Advanced HVAC: how airflow and humidity interact inside a growing room
  • Control approaches compared under the same conditions
03

Automation

exploring

Sensing, control and data logging that make runs repeatable.

  • Sensor and logging methods that someone else can repeat
  • Control approaches tested on a working farm
  • Data kept in a form that makes runs easy to compare
04

Energy

exploring

How much energy a run uses, and how that changes with the weather.

  • Energy measured next to climate, run by run
  • Where the biggest loads are, and what could lower them
  • Other sources of energy for a growing room
+

Beyond the boundaries

direction

We set no boundary on the questions. When something can be measured and made to work better, we explore it as a proof of concept first, and say so on this page when work starts.

running: experiments are underway. exploring: we are reading and testing ideas. direction: an open area with no fixed limit. Nothing is published yet. Results will be added here once they are checked.
