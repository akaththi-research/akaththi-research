How a question becomes research

From idea to working research.

We start small, prove an idea can work, and then test it again and again on a working farm. Nothing is published until it has been checked.

  1. 01Question

    Something we want to know, written down before we start.

  2. 02Experiment

    A small, controlled test with the readings logged.

  3. 03Proof of concept

    Does the idea work at all? We show it on a bench or a single run.

  4. 04Working research

    Repeated runs on a working farm, in real heat and humidity.

  5. 05Shared

    Protocols and data, published once they are checked.

How we work

Measure first

Every claim comes from logged data from a real run, repeated before we conclude anything.

Say what we do not know

Failed runs and negative results are recorded with the successful ones.

Test on a working farm

Results have to hold up outside a lab, in the heat and humidity of a real farm.

Have a question that needs a real growing space?

We work with universities, institutes, research firms and growers.
