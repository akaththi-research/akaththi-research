Akaththi Research · Chennai, India

Science for living systems.

We research anything that can be measured and made to work better. We research and explore mushroom cultivation in tropical climates like Chennai, tested on a working farm.

$focus: mushrooms, tropical

Projects

  • 01 Exotic oyster cultivation Growing exotic oyster mushrooms in the heat and humidity of Chennai. running
  • 02 King oyster Whether king oyster can be grown well in a tropical climate. exploring
  • 03 Cooling tech Keeping a growing room cool at low energy, where the weather is hot. exploring

running means experiments are underway. exploring means we are reading and testing ideas. Nothing is published yet.

How we work

Measure first

Every claim comes from logged data from a real run, repeated before we conclude anything.

Say what we do not know

Failed runs and negative results are recorded with the successful ones.

Test on a working farm

Results have to hold up outside a lab, in the heat and humidity of a real farm.
