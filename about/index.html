About Akaththi Research

Akaththi Research studies anything that can be measured and made to work better. We are based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and our focus now is mushroom cultivation in tropical climates.

How we work

  • Measure before we claim.
  • Record failed runs as carefully as good ones.
  • Test on a working farm, not only in a lab.
  • Say what exists, what is running and what is only planned.

Where things stand

Our first areas are planned, other fields are open to questions, and nothing is published yet. This site will carry protocols, datasets and papers as they are checked, and will say plainly when something is still a question.
