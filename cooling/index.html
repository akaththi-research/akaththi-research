Cooling

Holding cool, steady conditions in a small growing space, in a hot climate, without a large energy bill.

status
planned. No cooling results yet.
output
Measured comparisons of cooling methods, in energy per unit of growing space.

Questions we are asking

  • What is the least energy that holds a set temperature for a given crop in a hot, humid climate?
  • How do different cooling methods compare when measured the same way?
  • How does cooling interact with humidity and fresh air?
  • What happens in a heat wave or a power cut, and how long can a space ride it out?

How we measure

  • Energy and temperature logged together, for the same space and the same weather.
  • Real local weather, not a mild test day.
  • Costs counted in the local electricity tariff.
