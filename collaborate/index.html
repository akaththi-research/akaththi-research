Researchers, institutes and firms, in India and abroad.

What we bring

  • A working farm in Chennai to test on, in real heat and humidity.
  • Logged climate and growth data from each run. building
  • Written protocols, so a run can be repeated by someone else.

What we look for

  • Co-investigators on a shared question.
  • Methods we do not have, such as genetics, microbiology or analysis.
  • Students and visiting researchers who want data from a working farm.

Before work starts

We agree in writing what is being studied, who owns the data, who may publish, and who holds any rights that come out of it.

Findings that lead to a product may be published after they are protected.
